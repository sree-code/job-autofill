Appliqué: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026 (beta)

Appliqué is a browser extension with one purpose: filling job application forms with information you enter, so you don't type the same details on every application. This policy explains what data it handles and where that data goes.

Data stored on your device

Your profile (name, contact details, address, links, work and education history, eligibility and optional self-identification answers), your resume and cover letter, your settings, your AI API key and the AI session memory are stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local). The developer cannot access this data.

Data sent to the AI provider you choose (optional)

If you turn on AI answers and add your own API key, questions the extension can't answer from your profile are sent with your profile to the provider you selected (DeepSeek or Anthropic), using your key, over HTTPS. The answers come back to your browser. The provider's own privacy policy governs that request. The developer never receives it.

Anonymous usage metrics (required during the beta)

This is a beta release. To use it you must accept anonymous usage metrics: the first time you open the options page, the extension shows what is collected and asks you to click “Accept and continue”. Until you accept, the extension does not fill forms and sends nothing. If you don't want metrics, uninstall the extension; that stops all collection.

Sent after you acceptNever sent
A random install ID; extension and browser version; the job site's hostname (no page path); detected framework and applicant-tracking system; number of fields found and filled; the types of fields filled (for example “email”); question labels it couldn't match, with emails and numbers masked; fill and AI timings; AI provider and model; error messages. Your profile values, anything typed into forms, AI answers, API keys, resume or cover letter, full page URLs, browsing history.

Metrics are used only to improve fill accuracy, speed and reliability. They're stored in a private storage bucket that only the developer can access. IP addresses are not stored. A coarse country code from the hosting provider may be kept.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent data. We don't use data for advertising, profiling or creditworthiness. We don't let humans read your data except as aggregate metrics you accepted. We don't share data with third parties beyond the AI provider you pick yourself.

The use of information received from the extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Turn off AI answers at any time on the options page.
  • Stop metrics at any time by uninstalling the extension.
  • Remove all stored data by uninstalling the extension.
  • To have the metrics for your install deleted, open an issue with your install ID (shown in the extension's storage) at the project's GitHub page.
