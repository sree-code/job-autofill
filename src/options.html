Appliqué is in beta. To use it, you agree to send anonymous usage metrics: which job sites and form types it ran on, how many fields it filled, how long that took, and questions it couldn't match. Your profile, answers, API key and resume are never sent. Read the privacy policy. If you'd rather not, uninstall the extension.

Filling

When on, Appliqué fills an application as soon as the form appears. It never submits anything; you always click Submit yourself.

Job postings

On a job posting, a small Appliqué tab appears at the right edge and reads the posting on this computer. Saved jobs stay in this browser. With an API key, the posting text (not your profile) is also sent to your AI provider for a short summary. On LinkedIn nothing runs until you click.

Sites where Appliqué stays quiet

career-ops

Sends each posting to your career-ops folder through its local capture server (node capture-server.mjs), which adds it to jds/ and a Pending row in data/pipeline.md. Nothing is committed, pushed or evaluated. Only this computer (127.0.0.1) is contacted.

AI answers

Questions your profile doesn't cover are sent with your profile to the provider you pick, using your own key. The key stays in this browser.
